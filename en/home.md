# 🥳 Welcome to the Blabla Linux wiki!

## 👋 Hello everyone!

You may already know me from the [blog](https://yourls.blablalinux.be/blog). It's perfect for sharing **news and updates** about **Blabla Linux**, our community and free software.

However, the blog isn't the ideal medium for purely technical content. That's why I created this wiki: a space dedicated to the **accuracy** and **longevity** of installation methods.

## 🧠 The wiki's mission: efficiency first

Every day, I install and test many services. My goal with this wiki is to give you the **final working result**.

This wiki is my **public cheat sheet**, designed to be a reliable, ready-to-use source of documentation.

* **Why?** To offer you "turnkey" technical guides, regularly updated, so you can get a successful installation without wasting time.
* **The method:** Every guide here is the result of my own testing, distilled to give you only the most direct and effective steps.
* **Read-only:** To keep procedures consistent and reliable, I remain the sole author of the content.
